Researcher in medical image analysis and efficient vision

Mohammad Asif Hasan

{% include icon.html name="location" %}{{ a.degree }}, BUET, Dhaka

I build image analysis models that a clinician can inspect and question. My thesis studied breast cancer imaging end to end — segmenting lesions in ultrasound, removing the pectoral muscle from mammograms without erasing lesions, and predicting BI-RADS categories from images and clinical metadata together. I also work on lightweight RGB–infrared detectors that run on drones and single-board computers.

Research overview
{% include glyph.html name="lesion" %}

Medical image segmentation

Lesion segmentation in breast ultrasound and mammography, boundary-aware objectives, and metrics that capture clinical failure modes.

{% include glyph.html name="fusion" %}

Multimodal and ordinal learning

Cross-attention fusion of images with clinical metadata, and ordinal objectives for graded labels such as BI-RADS.

{% include glyph.html name="drone" %}
