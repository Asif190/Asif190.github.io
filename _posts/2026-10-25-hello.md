This is where I will write about the papers I read, the methods I use and the mistakes I make along the way, mostly in medical image analysis and efficient computer vision.

<!--more-->

Writing a result up for someone else is the fastest way I know to find the gaps in my own understanding. Expect posts such as:

- short reading notes on segmentation and multimodal fusion papers;
- explanations of evaluation choices, for example why overlap metrics alone can hide clinically important failures;
- practical notes on training and deploying models on small hardware.

Posts can include equations when they help. For example, the Dice similarity coefficient between a predicted mask $P$ and a ground truth $G$ is

$$
\mathrm{DSC}(P, G) = \frac{2\,|P \cap G|}{|P| + |G|}.
$$
